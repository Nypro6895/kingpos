The combined candidate already contains the November 18 legal revision and the
owner-approved advertising-credit refund process: Support reviews each purchase
under its actual terms and applicable law; stopping a campaign returns eligible
unused credit to the balance without automatically refunding the card. Statutory
refund rights remain reserved.

The original isolated copy-edit test still expected October metadata and rejected
every occurrence of “cash refund”, including the approved explanation that a
credit return and a cash refund are separate outcomes. On the combined source it
passed eight checks and failed those two stale assertions.

This change updates only that regression test and this integration note. It pins
the existing November 18 revision, retains the established August 21 effective
date, routes and section anchors, and checks the approved refund distinctions.

credit expiry remain. No policy wording, price, refund execution, age rule,
consent, provider setting or publication status changes.

The legal renderer, disclosures, ownership, platform duties, deletion/export,
children's requirements and provider-assurance regressions remain in the same
suite. Local passing tests do not establish hosted provider practices, completed
privacy operations, the legal entity, Store labels or a published policy. The
final release must still review its publication dates and notice/assent needs.
