REYLUMI — LOCAL-FIRST POS, STAFF AND CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE
2026-10-16 UTC

Purpose
Open a previously prepared authorized screen from device storage, keep work
while moving between tabs or losing connectivity, and reconcile changed live
data without rebuilding the entire screen. A first installation or a screen
that has never completed preparation still needs a connection. No zero-ms or
unmeasured physical-device speed claim is made.

POS and owner
Preserve the existing financial rules, draft versions, receipt checkpoints,
idempotent operation IDs and fresh server validation. Device recovery survives
a new iPhone WebView; another window cannot overwrite its active draft. Lost
responses retry the same operation. A submitted receipt does not reappear as
an unsent cart. Offline navigation opens complete prepared documents; warm
online tabs retain the existing workspace. Guarded navigation streams before
checkout data; checkout still waits for its complete authorized inputs.

Staff
My Work, workday and appointments use scoped partial reads. Protected disk
reads require an explicit owner or authorized staff-manager confirmation of
the current linked account in Staff. No historical link is silently approved.
An approval allows previously loaded own work/pay for at most 24 hours without
a fresh server check. Revocation applies on reconnect or lease expiry. Rebind,
deactivation and revision changes invalidate the prior authority. Staff draft
restoration or reconnection never silently submits a money/service change.

Security repair
Close participant direct-write access to staff connection requests. Preserve
legitimate manager writes and application/invitation decisions. New invitations
and owner resends receive protected provenance; historical pending invitations
need an owner resend before acceptance. Preserve all historical records and
existing online staff access. The migration does not change payroll formulas,
payment ledgers, account ownership or remove existing staff connections.

Customer
Retain authorized activity/history read results and prepared booking, saved
place, saved-post and following screens. Booking selections may be restored;
slot availability and confirmations are checked again. Payment details, tokens,
authentication/admin/edit pages and server actions are not generic cached data.
Prepared appointment, favorites, saved-post, following and receipt views update
quietly after fresh matching online authorization, at most once every 30 seconds;
active input, retained client drafts and scroll are preserved.

Web and app
Prepared pages include every required build script/style/font, including lazy
code. Publish a prepared page only after all required assets pass integrity and
completeness checks. Scope, device generation, permission and expiry checks run
before cached private markup can appear. Logout clears read snapshots and route
hints, while financial outboxes remain separately scoped and recoverable.
The iPhone app retains the last successfully prepared bare route. The Lumi
launch presentation and existing Safari payment/security flows are preserved.

Live data
Foreground/resume refreshes staff turns and income through existing partial
reads and wakes the existing POS outbox. iOS background refresh is best effort,
scheduled by iOS, and performs bounded authorized reads only. It is not a
continuous background connection and never submits financial writes. Native
snapshots are encrypted and excluded from backup; no session credential is
persisted in that snapshot store. Revoked staff offline permission is checked
again on background staff reads before any new private snapshot is stored.

Release evidence
Use the dated immutable change events, exact source hashes and final release
receipt for actual tests, deployment IDs and Apple/TestFlight availability.
A local build or higher app version alone is not publication evidence.
