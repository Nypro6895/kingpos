OWNER CAMPAIGNS — APPROVED WORKSPACE IMPLEMENTATION
2026-10-17 · implementation only; no production publication or real payment tests

Interface
- One edge-to-edge surface. Compact heading and icon row: Running, History,
  Settings, Create new. No enclosing card borders; status labels are plain text.
- History has exactly two structural rows per campaign: title/status, then
  location/date/credits with Details and the applicable action alongside.
- Running campaigns show counters and daily result bars. Results combine actual
  delivery events and attributed conversions; no example metrics are shipped.
- Supported delivery locations remain city, neighborhood, ZIP, state, country.
  The earlier preview's arbitrary radius is NOT claimed: delivery currently
  matches named places and does not have a verified viewer-coordinate contract.

Activation and creation
- Uninstalled: explanation and OFF switch. Switching opens the existing Market
  activation dialog. Campaigns remains free to install. Market opens the selected
  plugin and returns to Campaigns after installation.
- Installed: open the workspace directly. New photo/message or a published salon
  post; audience, duration, view ceiling, explicit credit ceiling and review.
- Run again creates a new campaign with a new id, reuses choices, revalidates the
  source/current settings, and preserves the previous results and history.
- Lower credit ceilings stop delivery early. Each future period must reserve
  its own credits; automatic buying cannot raise a campaign's chosen ceiling.

Credit denomination and billing
- $1 = 12 displayed credits. Existing integer storage remains unchanged:
  one old unit = 0.12 displayed credits. This preserves every balance, welcome
  grant, pack price, ad-delivery price and previously authorized monthly limit.
- No destructive balance conversion, price change, new subscription, currency
  change or automatic-purchase opt-in is performed by this task.
- Currency is displayed only inside Payment method and the explicit automatic
  purchase confirmation. Campaigns, creation and credit history show credits.
- Pack purchases, thresholds and monthly credit limits retain original server
  validation and locking. Consent defaults OFF. Saved cards belong to their
  authorizing owner. New periods need sufficient reserved credits.
- A deliberate start with automatic purchases on may queue funding. It never
  means that payment or review has succeeded. Stopping automatic purchases
  cancels only unclaimed automatic orders and clears pending ad funding intents.
- Block payments blocks new Reylumi requests, not the bank card itself. An
  already processing payment may finish and still grants purchased credits.
- Card setup is hosted by Stripe, verified against owner/salon and provider
  completion; only a masked summary reaches the interface. Revision checks stop
  stale checkouts or old setup tabs from replacing a newer/blocked card.
- Provider callbacks also complete setup independently of Safari login cookies.
  Replacing a card keeps automatic purchases OFF until explicitly reauthorized.
- Cancelled manual checkout is expired at the provider before its pending order
  is released. Return URL parameters alone never prove purchase success.

Notifications
- Low-credit reminders are optional and suppressed while automatic buying is on.
- Automatic purchase success/failure and reaching the monthly cap have deduped
  in-app notifications. Campaigns nearing the chosen credit cap can remind the
  owner to review a next period; adding credits does not expand the cap.
- Notification links select the correct owner salon workspace.
- Existing scheduler cadence remains 15 minutes. Funding may therefore wait for
  the next authorized worker pass. No new background dispatch is started here.

Validation evidence

  ownership, ceiling changes, refunds, idempotency, blocked charges, monthly
  limits, delayed payment/card setup, actual daily conversions and role grants.
- Browser tests render the real components with isolated server-action fixtures
  at 320/390/1280px and exercise create/run again, insufficient credits, consent,
