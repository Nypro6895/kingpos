SERVICE RELAY — IMPLEMENTATION RECEIPT

Scope
One work-in-progress ticket per customer visit. Staff accept/decline POS offers,
send individual service/price lines, and complete their own work. Sequential
staff share the same customer ticket. Cashier Submit delegates to the existing
Owner/Portable receipt core, which continues to own settlement, commission,
turn records and staff income.

Entry points
Market: Service Relay (initial free listing; no billing integration).
Staff: My Work / Daily.
Owner and Portable POS: Service tickets drawer.
Customer screen: QR for phone check-in and a digital pass after check-in.
Phone: /check-in/[entry], /visit/[pass]. Signed-in profiles prefill name/phone.

Use active, POS-enabled staff connected to an account and their existing
staff_service_assignments. Existing check-in settings determine eligibility.
Offers expire after 60 seconds and move to another eligible staff member.
If everyone declines/expires, staff can use Find customer and claim manually.
No service selection means automatic dispatch waits; manual claiming remains
available. Existing print-receipt functionality is independent of this plugin.

POS integration
Pending service lines are projected onto the POS queue using its configured
large-turn threshold, separately from official submitted turn parts. Accepting
a customer never adds one turn. POS Submit writes the official receipt and
records through the existing receipt function. Ordinary POS carts cannot create
a second ticket for a customer with accepted Service Relay work.
Uninstall is blocked until accepted tickets have been finished and settled.

Reliability and permissions
Salon-scoped staff identity, service scope validation, optimistic revisions,
one active offer per customer/staff, transaction locks, idempotent settlement,
local preservation of unsent edits, realtime hints and visible-page polling.
Notifications are in the existing app notification center, not OS push/SMS.
Public passes expire after 24 hours and exclude phone numbers and price lines.
Phone check-in QR entry links expire after 8 hours and never expose the paired
display's checkout token. Camera QR scan depends on BarcodeDetector support;
manual number/link entry is always available. Screen wake lock is optional.
No phone proximity pairing, automatic SMS or native-app store build is included.
