Customer flow
- Personal bottom Notifications becomes QR; existing notifications remain available.
- My QR initially shows the opaque Personal ID. No Staff connection tab is added.
- Scan the paired salon check-in screen QR, choose services and Check in using
  the signed-in account. Do not repeat the customer's own identity/contact fields.
- Existing anonymous Phone Checkin stays available when that plugin is installed.
  Check-in Receipt alone enables authenticated Personal check-in, without
  enabling anonymous Phone Checkin as a side effect.
- Each visit provides its existing check-in QR for Staff. Staff may also scan
  the Personal ID when exactly one unpaid visit exists in their own salon.
- POS can send Ready with optional staff/chair; the app notification opens the
  exact visit. Ready is neither Working nor a financial/turn mutation.
- Show requested services, Working staff and saved amounts. Refresh visible
  status every five seconds and on resume/reconnect; offline keeps last saved
  values with a clear indicator, without submitting new check-ins offline.
- After settlement, show the same canonical closed receipt used by History,
  including tip, discount and tax. Expire the visit QR; retain Personal ID.
  History and View receipt link to existing Activity and receipt routes.
- Teal is primary; orange is reserved for Ready and important status accents.
  New interface text follows the existing English/Vietnamese mode.

Owner adds Staff
- Add Staff offers Scan personal QR. After scan, the manual forms are hidden;
  only the scanned user's name, masked contacts and Cancel / Send remain.
- Cancel has no write. Send reuses a unique verified-contact unlinked staff
  profile, preserving that profile's history, or prepares a pending profile.
  Ambiguous or unverified-contact collisions stop without merging accounts.
- Sending twice reuses the existing pending request. It does not immediately
  give Staff/POS access. Existing staff_salon_connection_requests notification
  and Accept/Decline logic completes the connection, with the same profile ID.
- Personal ID is an expiring opaque identifier, not a credential. Account,
  salon and server permissions are checked at each action.
