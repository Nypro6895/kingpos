## Findings that determine the next step

1. **Reviewed/reported photos can remain publicly retrievable after admin deletion.** The canonical action and admin decision soft-delete Beauty or delete a Salon post row (`20261017194500_post_actions_canonical_caption.sql:41`; `202610070007_post_safety.sql:169`). The new cleanup deliberately refuses *any* matching safety case, even resolved, and records `review_evidence` (`20261118220000_public_post_media_deletion.sql:53`, `:223`). Admin evidence is still the original object: `lib/platform-admin/post-safety.ts:14` and `:36` create one-hour signed URLs for public Beauty/Salon buckets. These are not private escrow copies. This is a documented remaining scope gap, not a newly discovered regression in that cleanup patch.
2. **Generic Support attachments are private from the public, but shared within the conversation.** `get_support_conversation` returns all attachments to the reporter or permitted admin (`20261008233001_review_case_communication.sql:288`); `lib/support-cases.ts:12` signs them for 600 seconds. Guest access has a separate cookie check and signs the same bucket (`lib/guest-support.ts:12`). `app/support/actions.ts:42` checks case access before upload. Automatically registering internal moderation evidence as a Support attachment could disclose it to the complainant/guest. Do not reuse that collection as internal-only escrow.
3. **A row snapshot is not a byte-preservation receipt.** `get_admin_post_review_workspace` obtains current Beauty media rows or a Salon snapshot's path (`202610070015_review_search_reason_labels.sql:34`). There is no immutable private evidence object/checksum/capture receipt associated with the case. Source changes, missing legacy files and account erasure can remove the material the reviewer expects: personal erasure deletes Beauty media and clears authored case snapshots (`202610140003_account_erasure.sql:108`, `:121`). Retained support/business files and a legal preservation hold are different purposes; neither justifies retaining every case forever.
4. **Existing reconsideration is primarily the reporter's workflow.** Reopen requires an authorized reviewer, a user-origin case and new reporter workflow state; it does not restore content (`20261008233001_review_case_communication.sql:375`). The post-review UI can contact the author, but that is not a formal author/copyright counter-notice lifecycle (`app/(app)/admin/post-safety/case-card.tsx:18`, `:56`). Generic Support resolution is not a content decision. Admin r2 and exact-text Hide/Restore remain proposals, not controls available to operators.

## Legal and platform requirements to operationalize

- Apple 1.2 requires filtering, reporting with timely handling, user blocking and accessible contact, and expects remediation after notice. Apple 5.2 requires appropriate rights to content. This is broader than a report receipt or unflagged classifier result. No Apple-fixed 24/72-hour response deadline is inferred. [Apple Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content)
- A general IP reason and Contact Support are useful intake, not proof of a compliant DMCA notice/counter-notice process or safe-harbor eligibility. If pursuing applicable §512 protection, counsel/owner must verify qualifying conditions, designated-agent/public contact, repeat-infringer policy and notice handling. A copyright notice generally needs claimant authority/signature, work and material/location identification, contact and required good-faith/accuracy statements; do not demand copyright proof for unrelated privacy/safety complaints. [Copyright Office §512 resources](https://www.copyright.gov/512/)
- Copyright counter-notices are distinct from ordinary appeals. §512(g) includes forwarding/notice and conditional restoration after 10–14 **business** days, subject to the statutory court-action exception; it is not an automatic timer for every report. Misrepresentation and repeat-infringer conditions also require a real policy/legal process. No Reylumi registration, eligibility, recipient service or legal response capability was verified. [17 USC §512 text](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html#512), [agent renewal requirements](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html)
- Negative criticism is not itself infringement. Fair use is fact-specific; neither the complainant's label nor the model decides ownership/fair use. [Copyright Office Fair Use Index](https://copyright.gov/fair-use/index.html)
- Keep an urgent nonconsensual-intimate-image lane separate from copyright. FTC guidance says covered platforms need a notice/removal process and removal plus reasonable efforts for known identical copies within 48 hours of a valid request, effective May 19, 2026. Whether Reylumi meets the statutory covered-platform definition needs an actual legal assessment; do not assume it either way. Do not require an account or copyright ownership before escalating a victim's report. [FTC business guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-take-it-down-act)
- Public-bucket retrieval bypasses per-object read controls. Changing app rows or signing an original public URL does not privatize it. Private buckets need authenticated access or bearer signed links. [Supabase bucket access model](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/buckets/fundamentals) Smart CDN invalidation follows object changes, but browser copies and previously downloaded material remain outside recall. Manual purge is privileged and plan-dependent; project availability has not been checked. [Smart CDN](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/cdn/smart-cdn), [purge API](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/cdn/purge-cdn-cache)

## Proposed operations and implementation

### Separate three decisions

Keep **claim intake**, **public restriction/removal**, and **private evidence retention/access** as distinct audited states. A report never becomes automatic removal/approval; a resolved case never becomes automatic restoration. Ordinary author deletion keeps the conservative shared-reference rules. An authorized rights/privacy restriction must not remain public forever merely because a cover, Market selection, booking snapshot or review case references it.

Proposed operator record: case/category and receipt time; assigned reviewer/backup; request completeness and claimed authority; canonical source/version and exact affected uses; policy/legal basis; separate public response/internal reason; removal scope; capture state; origin/cache checkpoints; appeal/counter-notice deadlines where applicable; retention purpose/review date/hold authority. Store legal contact details privately and disclose only the required, reviewed subset to the other party. Do not turn an unsuccessful claim into an automatic account suspension. Existing suspension requires its separate authority.

### Private evidence, not reporter-visible Support attachments

Use a dedicated **private** rights/moderation evidence collection and bucket, or a demonstrably equivalent separately protected partition. Do not insert internal copies into `support_case_attachments`. No direct anon/user reads, listing, upload, signing or mutation. Derive source keys from an authorized canonical case; never let an operator client choose an arbitrary Storage path or ask the server to fetch an arbitrary claimant URL.

Proposed evidence row: opaque case/evidence IDs; exact source bucket/path plus pinned object ID/version/eTag, byte size/MIME/SHA-256; captured text/publication revision; private object ID/version/hash; capture actor/time/result; purpose, review/expiry policy reference and any justified preservation hold. Preserve the exact published bytes; label redacted thumbnails as derivatives. A filename or row JSON is not an integrity receipt. Missing/changed legacy sources are recorded as unavailable or needing a fresh review, not falsely captured.

New read/review capabilities should be explicit and case-scoped, not salon membership or blanket financial/workspace authority. A protected server stream with current per-request authorization and `no-store` is preferable to giving a complainant a reusable internal evidence URL. Any required short-lived signed link is still a bearer grant; its lifetime is not retention, and a role change alone must not be represented as immediate token/cache revocation. Audit evidence access without logging image bytes, secrets or unnecessary legal PII. No automatic external AI transfer of complaint/evidence data is authorized by the public-post consent.

### Recoverable removal sequence

| Stage | Required invariant/checkpoint |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Authorize and scope | Active allowed reviewer, case permission, current evidence/source revision, required reason and a request-ID/fingerprint receipt. Enumerate exact assets and shared uses; no prefix/bucket purge. Immediately hide/restrict app publication when the authorized urgency decision requires it. |
| 2. Freeze/capture | Queue a pinned capture job without holding a DB transaction across Storage calls. Prevent new public reattachment/recreation while processing. Service reads the selected exact bytes, uploads an immutable private copy with no upsert, reads/verifies it, then records its actual identity/hash. A timeout or collision never becomes `evidence_ready`. |
| 3. Resolve references | Reviewed per-use outcomes: remove public display or replace with a safe placeholder; retain lawful private business/history content through separately authorized access where needed. Preserve POS/payroll/Tax/bank records. A copyright complaint is not a reason to bulk erase financial history. Covers/copies/Market references need explicit treatment, not silent orphaning. |
| 4. Remove origin | A dedicated rights-removal authorization requires a verified private receipt or an explicit exceptional decision recording unavailable evidence. Revalidate current source identity, reference dispositions, holds and lease. Use Storage API for exact-object removal, then SQL absence checkpoint. Never delete `storage.objects` directly. |
| 5. Verify delivery | Record origin absence separately from edge invalidation/purge and public/transform/previous-link readback. Treat delayed/unknown cache results as pending, not completed. Do not promise browser/download/third-party recall. |
| 6. Resolve or reconsider | Acknowledge the actual outcome, preserve a private case trail and required notification/counter-notice steps. An appeal remains hidden until a separately authorized decision satisfies current eligibility. No automatic old-path revival. |

Urgent unlawful/sensitive content must not remain publicly served indefinitely solely because preservation failed. An authorized escalation can remove/disable with an explicit evidence-loss/unavailable receipt and approved handling. Suspected illegal sexual material involving children requires a specialized lawful handling/escalation process; do not make a routine Support copy or submit it to general AI classification.

This is a multi-step transaction with durable receipts, not a single cross-service DB transaction. Use unique `(case, source revision, asset identity, action)` and request fingerprint; immutable private keys; service-only bounded claims with lease tokens; monotonic checkpoint transitions. Lost copy/delete/finish responses retry the same identity. A different current object is a conflict, never a deletion target. Expired/stale tokens cannot complete another attempt. Cleanup of abandoned private captures requires the approved retention policy, not a new unbounded sweep.

Reuse the established user → sorted manifest → job lock order. Source/reference/asset rows already held by other writers need `NOWAIT`/bounded retry rather than a reverse wait under the manifest lock. Recheck shared references with fresh snapshots. Existing account-erasure ownership and `retain_business` COPY checks remain intact; do not steal leases or change their financial retention authority.

**Current fence compatibility must be designed explicitly:** `aa_public_media_reference` also guards `post_safety_cases` updates (`20261118220000_public_post_media_deletion.sql:150`, `:162`). Simply deleting a reviewed public object and setting the existing fence could then block case updates that still reference that path. Separate immutable evidence-only linkage from new publication references; permit authorized case metadata changes against verified private escrow without reacquiring a public attachment. Keep the permanent old-public-path fence. Do not globally disable the case/reference guard or merely delete the review case to bypass cleanup exclusion.

### Restoration and retention are policy decisions, not defaults

Recommended technical invariant: old removed public keys stay permanently fenced. If content restoration is allowed after the proper appeal/counter-notice process, create a new reviewed publication revision/object key and attestation, preserving the old decision/evidence link. Check active author, current source intent, privacy/withdrawal, all other holds and rights decisions. Never resurrect an author-deleted/private post or release another restriction as a side effect of resolving this case. A permanent key fence is not necessarily a permanent ban on the content; the owner/legal process must resolve that distinction.

Do not pick a retention duration from this source audit. Each private evidence purpose needs an approved period/review rule, deletion job, hold authority and release process. Account deletion and business retention need explicit purpose-based integration; today's personal erasure must not silently defeat a lawful hold, and “any prior case” must not create indefinite private retention. No automatic historical backfill or transfer of legacy public files is proposed.
