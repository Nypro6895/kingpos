Check-in Receipt — By Reylumi
Price: USD $4.70 per salon per month.

Flow: Customer enters their information and confirms requested services with Done.
The confirmation issues one daily queue number per visit and prints a receipt with
the customer's name and a QR identifier. Staff opens POS > Waiting / Customers,
scans with a USB/Bluetooth QR scanner that types into the scan field, and starts
the customer's ticket using the existing POS workflow. The scan does not charge
the customer or close a ticket. Existing service, staff and payment rules apply.

Activation requires migration 202610080007_checkin_receipt_plugin.sql, deployment
of the web changes, and a rebuilt desktop installer. Market Stripe credentials
and the existing /api/market/payment-webhook must be connected. Successful
subscription confirmation activates the plugin; redirect parameters do not.
No customer payment is taken by the check-in receipt flow.

Windows: use the desktop's paired customer display, set an 80 mm receipt printer
as the Windows default, and install the updated desktop version. Browser devices
open a printer dialog. Camera scanning is not included; use a QR-capable scanner.
Failed printing can be retried from Print again. Queue numbers reset on the
salon's local business date and are retained on reprint. Automatic reprints are
suppressed in the running desktop session. Printing requires an online check-in.

Verification: database entitlement/isolation/retry/daily reset tests, billing
amount and ownership tests, and native receipt escaping/validation tests.
A physical printer and scanner still need an on-device acceptance check.
