Booking Deposit — By Reylumi — USD $5.90/month per salon

Owner workflow
Market > Booking Deposit: subscribe, connect the salon's own Stripe account, set an amount and enable the requirement.
The same settings are included in All Settings > Booking.
Choose fixed USD deposit, percentage (up to 100%), or full service price.
Choose card entry in booking, Stripe-hosted checkout, or let customers choose.
Turning the requirement off preserves settings, billing and receipts. Manage subscription opens Stripe billing separately.
Salon pays Stripe processing, refund and dispute fees. No additional application fee is added to customer payments.

Customer workflow
Review services and time, see amount due now and estimated balance, choose checkout and pay.
Stripe handles card fields. Reylumi never receives raw card data.
Successful, server-verified payment creates the appointment using existing scheduling and notification rules.
If scheduling rejects the paid appointment, a database reservation prevents later confirmation and requests a full refund.
Payments under Stripe's USD $0.50 minimum cannot proceed; customers are directed to contact the salon.
Receipt and paid/refunded amounts are visible in booking details, customer history and the salon's customer record.
Appointment cancellation does not automatically refund a deposit; salon owners process refunds in their Stripe dashboard.
Refund webhooks synchronize the recorded refunded amount.

Required configuration before live use
Apply supabase/migrations/20261009140031_booking_deposit_plugin.sql.
BOOKING_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY: Stripe Connect platform secret key.
NEXT_PUBLIC_BOOKING_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY: matching publishable key, present at build time.
BOOKING_STRIPE_CONNECT_CLIENT_ID: OAuth client ID for connecting salon Stripe accounts.
Register https://reylumi.com/api/booking-payment/connect as an allowed OAuth redirect.
BOOKING_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET: Connect webhook signing secret for /api/booking-payment/webhook.
Enable connected-account events: checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded,
checkout.session.expired, charge.refunded, account.application.deauthorized.
MARKET_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and MARKET_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET plus enabled Market payment settings are required for the monthly subscription.
Keep existing Market webhook events, including subscription lifecycle and invoice events, enabled.

Use Stripe sandbox to verify each checkout method, SCA challenge, cancellation, retry, expiration,
slot conflict with refund, duplicate webhooks, partial/full dashboard refund, and plugin subscription expiry.
No live charge is needed for these tests.
