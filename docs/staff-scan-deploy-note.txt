STAFF SCAN — 2026-10-25
Change ID: staff-scan-20261025
Status: implementation complete locally; NOT DEPLOYED; needs integration with current production source.

Approved flow
- Staff bottom Notifications tab becomes Scan; the header notification bell remains.
- Scan QR or tap an unpaid check-in, with no confirmation screen.
- Show requested services from check-in. Tap to select, enter separate amounts,
  or find/add another service enabled for this staff member in the salon catalog.
- Connecting immediately establishes Working + Holding. Stand by keeps that
  connection without requiring an amount. Cancel releases a new empty connection;
  on saved work Cancel only discards current unsent edits.
- Finish amount entry (keyboard completion, leaving the editor, or Stand by with
  valid amounts) to save to POS and return to Scan. Saving alone does not finish work.
- Tap the current-work card to edit or Done. Done projects large/small turns once.
  Holding reserves queue position without counting completed service turns.
- POS can edit staff, services and amounts and collect payment before phone Done.
  POS settlement uses the existing receipt/earnings/commission/accounting core.
- POS ticket linkage is authoritative. Reconnect removes settled work and its
  draft before any retry. Old revisions cannot reopen or overwrite settled work.
- Local unsent edits are not visible to POS. They are retained in scoped session
  drafts while work is open and are never automatically replayed after reconnect.
- English remains the default; new Staff UI copy follows the installed Vietnamese mode.
- Teal/green is the main page color. Orange is limited to Holding and Done emphasis.

Implementation and dependencies
- New migration: supabase/migrations/20261025170000_staff_scan_workspace.sql.
  Requires the existing Check-in Receipt tables and Service Relay migrations
  through 20261009150036 (plus their installed later patches).
- No new plugin purchase is required: an active Check-in Receipt subscription OR
  installed Phone Checkin enables new connections. Existing accepted work can
  still be completed if entitlement expires.
- Explicit scan tickets bypass automatic service-relay dispatch. Legacy automatic
  offers and their accounting behavior remain available.
- QR formats: reylumi:checkin:<visit UUID>, or an HTTPS reylumi.com/visit/<pass UUID>.
  Both resolve within the current salon and authenticated staff identity.
- Added jsqr 1.4.0, lazily loaded only when Scan opens. Frames stay on the device.
- Personal QR is now implemented by personal-qr-20261025. Include its QR provider,
  check-in/POS Ready/history flow and qr-full-flow-20261025 fixes in the same release.
- No native files or bridge contracts changed. Web implementation only. Verify
  real Safari/WKWebView camera permissions, keyboard and resume behavior on iPhone
  before release; native changes/build only if that verification requires them.
