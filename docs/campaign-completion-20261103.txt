1. COMPLETED BEHAVIOR

- Automatic purchase consent uses the same displayed-credit -> stored-unit
  conversion as the submitted limit. 3,600 displayed credits = 3,000 cents = $30.
  Invalid intermediate input cannot crash the consent form.
- Credit pack prices are visible before payment. Copy distinguishes returning
  unused campaign credits to the salon wallet from refunding a bank card.
- A signed click is accepted independently of a billable view. It never creates
  a view or changes spend, wallet balances or the credit ledger.
- Click admission retains service-only RPC access, visitor/account binding,
  same-origin API protection, approval, time, content, ownership and geographic
  checks. Existing one-unique-click-per-visitor/campaign semantics are retained.
  Replay acknowledges the existing click without extending attribution time.
- The view admission RPC, prices, rounding, caps, pacing and wallet rules remain
  unchanged. The final budget-exhausting view does not swallow its CTA click.
- Sponsored cards show verified business name/location when available. Long
  titles and captions are clamped; full creative remains on the destination.
  Ratings, discounts, available times and audience forecasts are not invented.
- The approved custom destination remains readable through the remaining scheduled
  period after budget completion. Cancelled/hidden/archived/ineligible sources
  remain unavailable. Its booking CTA uses canonical booking readiness; otherwise
  the business profile/contact path remains available.
- Campaigns replaces the misleading Running tab. Zero results are not described
  as customers noticing the salon. Per-campaign text states whether credits,
  review, an owner action or settlement is pending.
- Funding controls say Reserve & submit for unapproved ads and Start approved ad
  for approved reruns. Automatic-purchase permission remains separate.
- Creation review shows state/country qualifiers, both credits and USD, and an
  explicit submission action. Unverified illustrative conversion numbers are
  removed from the intro. Existing renewal choices and advanced controls remain.
- Detail reports add daily unique clicks, checked-at/last-view timestamps and
  current eligibility reasons without mutating accounting on read.
- Lifetime advertising value used, value per recorded view and value per
  attributed booked customer are shown. Zero denominators show Not available.
  Promotional value is explicitly distinguished from paid cash, revenue/profit.
- Historical attribution is retained while linked booking evidence is categorized
  by its current completed/cancelled/other/unavailable state.
- Detail polling reloads the selected campaign even after it leaves the open
  campaigns list. Stale report responses cannot overwrite a changed selection.
- 7/30/90-day charts include today and label multi-day bins as date ranges.

2. METRIC CONTRACT AND BOUNDARIES

Views: existing qualifying, admitted exposures; not unique people.
Unique clicks: at most one per existing visitor identity and campaign. Early
clicks need not have a billable view, so clicks/views is not sold as CTR.
Customers who booked: existing deduplicated customer attribution after a
signed-in click within seven days. It is not the total number of appointments.
Original attribution remains historical if the booking is cancelled afterward.
Booking outcomes describe the current state of the linked original evidence;
unavailable evidence is not treated as zero completed bookings.
No booking-start metric or guest-to-login identity bridge is fabricated.
No revenue, collected payment or ROAS is inferred from a booking.
No new identifiers, sensitive tracking, prices or card-debit permissions are added.

REFUND/DISPUTE POLICY:
Automatic card refunds, chargeback recovery and deduction of already-spent
credits are NOT implemented or silently introduced by this change. Their
financial policy needs an explicit owner decision and dedicated provider tests.
Until then, handle these as a manual reviewed financial operation:
verify the provider transaction/refund/dispute and corresponding order; inspect
available/reserved/spent credits and any processing automatic order; preserve
ledger/history; agree the actual action and customer communication before
moving money or changing purchased entitlements. Reconciliation of a successful
payment is not evidence that its later refund/dispute has been resolved.
The existing payment-block control is available to an authorized operator;
a processing payment may still finish.

No commercial completeness, delivery guarantee, card-refund automation or
production acceptance is implied by the local ready event.
